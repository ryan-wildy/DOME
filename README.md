# DOME GeM seller workspace

This repository contains the earlier DOME authorization workspace. The current DOME platform helps Indian suppliers find live GeM tenders, research what government buyers purchase and connect with OEMs and resellers.

## Seller resources

- [Browse live GeM tenders by product and delivery state](https://the-dome.in/opportunities)
- [Research printer-consumables buyers, purchase history and open bids](https://the-dome.in/opportunities/toner-cartridges-ink-cartridges-consumables-for-printers-a91c011f)
- [Read the guide to OEM authorization for GeM resellers](https://the-dome.in/learn/oem-authorization)

DOME is an independent research and collaboration platform. Check bid documents and submit your bid on the [Government e-Marketplace](https://gem.gov.in/).
